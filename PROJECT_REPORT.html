1. Executive Summary

In retail and omni-channel commerce, inventory mismanagement drives significant financial drag through two major channels: stockouts (lost immediate revenue and customer churn) and overstocking (tied-up working capital, elevated holding costs, and margin degradation via forced clearance markdowns).

Project FORESIGHT is an end-to-end predictive analytics and decision-support system built for NorthBay Living across 12 high-velocity everyday home product SKUs over a 2-year transactional horizon. The system delivers an automated ETL pipeline, a 6-week rolling time-series forecast model using HistGradientBoostingRegressor (14.5% WAPE, a 19.7% accuracy boost over seasonal-naive benchmarks), a risk-scoring matrix at a 90% service level, and an interactive executive planning dashboard deployed on Render.

Key Business Impact: Flagged ₹7.28 Lakhs in sales-at-risk across 5 stockout-prone products and identified ₹2.14 Lakhs in surplus working capital trapped in overstocked goods, providing actionable replenishment quantities directly to operations.

2. Problem Statement & Objectives

NorthBay Living manages diverse catalog lines spanning Kitchenware, Bedding, Home Decor, and Small Appliances with supplier lead times between 7 and 30 days. Operations teams lacked unified tools to project demand across marketing events or quantify the financial stakes of reordering too late vs. buying too much.

Core Deliverables:

  • D1 (Data Pipeline & Hygiene): Ingest and clean 4 transactional datasets (duplicates, missing prices, string anomalies) and create an indexed SQLite database.
  • D3 (Machine Learning Forecast): Predict SKU-level demand for the next 6 weeks using strictly leak-free historical, calendar, and promotional features.
  • D4 (Risk Scoring & Decisions): Categorize inventory into a 4-quadrant decision matrix (Reorder Now, Markdown / Clear, Watch, Healthy) with monetary risk estimation.
  • D5 (Web Dashboard & Security): Develop an interactive, role-based planning tool with PBKDF2 cryptographic password hashing and deploy to cloud production.

3. Machine Learning Demand Forecasting

The forecasting engine predicts weekly sales for H = 6 weeks ahead. To avoid lookahead bias, all features use only information available up to the forecast origin date:

  • last1: Immediate prior week demand.
  • m4, m8: 4-week and 8-week moving averages of demand velocity.
  • ly, ly3: Demand from identical week last year (t-52) and centered 3-week window.
  • promo, hol: Future known promotional events and official public holiday counts.
  • woy: ISO calendar week of the year to capture annual seasonality.

4. Inventory Risk Scoring & Decision Matrix

Safety stock is dynamically sized for a 90% Cycle Service Level (Z = 1.28) considering weekly demand volatility (σ₁₃) and supplier lead times:

$$\text{Safety Stock} = 1.28 \times \sigma_{13} \times \sqrt{\text{Lead Time Weeks}}$$

Total replenishment need is compared against supply (On Hand + On Order) to compute the Cover Ratio and Weeks of Supply, populating the 4-box decision grid:
